With Sort on "My rank", grab the six-dot grip to drag players into your own order — the ranking sticks across refreshes. Tiers: filter to a single position (Sort on "My rank") and hit "+ add tier break" to split that position into quality classes. Breaks are draggable dividers — a tier holds however many players you put between them, and each position can have its own count (up to 12 tiers). "Split" halves a tier, "merge ↑" removes a break, and dragging a player across a break re-tiers him without moving anyone else. Sold players stay counted in their tier, so headers show what's left live — and the advisor warns you when a player is the last of his tier. Tiers ride sync and Export/Import like everything else. Use "+ mark sold" on an unsold player to log a sale by hand before Sleeper reports it; it clears itself once Sleeper confirms the pick. The magnifier next to Player searches by name — it kicks in at 2 letters and looks across sold and unsold alike, ignoring the availability and status chips. Sync links two browsers to one shared board (tags, notes, rank, overrides, manual sales, team budgets — last edit wins, ~3s lag): both people paste the same private sync code once, and it reconnects automatically on every load. If sync ever drops, the page just runs local-only. Export copies your whole board as a backup code; Import loads one (and, if sync is on, makes it the shared board).
